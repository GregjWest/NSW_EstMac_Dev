Changelog

1.1.0 (August 9th, 2023)

Added

Added a new Projected Wetlands tab for sea level rise (SLR) projected modelling of mangrove and saltmarsh communities under 2 SLR scenarios; present day and 0.5m sea level increase.

Added links to two new papers on the About tab;

Glasby, T.M., Gibson, P.T., Laird, R., Swadling, D.S. and West, G. (2023), Black summer bushfires caused extensive damage to estuarine wetlands in New South Wales, Australia. Ecol Manag Restor,

Hughes MG, Glasby TM, Hanslow DJ, West GJ, Wen L. (2022). Random Forest Classification Method for Predicting Intertidal Wetland Migration Under Sea Level Rise. Frontiers in Environmental Science 10.

Updated

Updated mapping for Batemans Bay, Clyde River and Cullendulla Creek

Updated

Updated estuarine macrophyte symbology

1.0.16 (January 16th, 2023)

Added

New mapping has been added for Tweed River, Cudgen Creek, Cudgera Creek and Mooball Creek. This mapping includes up-to-date estuarine macrophytes, artificial structures and damage to mangrove and saltmarsh.

Added a link to a new paper; Swadling DS, West GJ, Gibson PT, Laird RJ, Glasby TM. (2022). Don't go breaking apart: Anthropogenic disturbances predict meadow fragmentation of an endangered seagrass. Aquatic Conservation: Marine and Freshwater Ecosystems on the About tab
