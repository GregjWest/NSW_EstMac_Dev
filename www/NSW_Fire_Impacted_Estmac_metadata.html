Title

Fire impacted Estuarine Macrophytes of NSW

Abstract

Represents an estimate of areas of mangrove and saltmarsh damaged in affected estuaries due to the 2020 NSW bushfires. Imagery was sourced from Nearmap and fire damage was mapped using eCognition. The layer may contain omissions or additional damage not present.

Purpose

This data was captured as part of an ongoing mapping program for monitoring changes in the distribution and extent of seagrass, mangrove and saltmarsh in NSW estuaries.

Lineage

Data Collection Method: Aerial photography interpretation. Areas affected by the fires were broadly identified where the GEEBAM layer intersected with mangroves and saltmarsh from the NSW Estuarine Macrophytes layer (NSWEML). Nearmap Imagery was sourced for these areas at 60cm/pixel resolution. Obvious areas damaged by fire (blackened or absent) were mapped. Some additional areas adjacent to but outside the NSWEML were also included. Mapping was completed using Object Based Image Analysis (OBIA). Images were segmented and affected segments containing burnt mangroves or saltmarsh identified (in some instances Phragmities was included with saltmarsh). These damaged areas were then exported out as ESRI shapefiles where areas were calculated in square metres.

 

Data Set Source: Orthorectified imagery sourced from Nearmap.  

 

Estuaries mapped: Berrara Creek, Burrill Lake, Camden Haven River, Candlagan Creek, Clyde River, Durras Lake, Fisheries Creek, Kappinghat Creek, Lake Innes and Lake Cathie, Lake Tabourie, Manning River, Narrawallee Inlet, Nullica River, Tomaga River, Towamba River, Wallis Lake and Wonboyn Lake.

Attribute accuracy

Burnt vegetation boundaries were identified on ortho-rectified aerial photographs. Mapped areas have not been formally field validated. Some areas have been confirmed by external sources.
